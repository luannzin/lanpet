# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

desktop

Native desktop app (Rust, eframe/egui 0.36, immediate-mode custom-painted UI) shipped as installers for Linux (.deb, X11/XWayland), Windows and macOS, updating itself from GitHub Releases. Undecorated, transparent, always-on-top windows; no web runtime.

## Users

Office coworkers on the same LAN. LanPet runs all day beside their real work: glanced at between tasks, cared for in short bursts, and social when coworkers' pets end up in the same room.

## Product Purpose

A tiny pixel pet that lives on your desktop. It studies, lifts, runs, sleeps and explores on timers while you work (and keeps progressing while the app is closed), then hangs out with coworkers' pets over the LAN: pets in the same room see each other, chat, wave, trade gifts and battle. Success is a pet people keep open all day without it competing with their work, and small shared moments with coworkers.

## Positioning

Zero-setup LAN presence: no accounts, no server. Anyone on the same network running LanPet is discovered automatically, and the room your pet is in is the social space.

## Operating Context

- Lives next to work apps; must stay out of the way until looked at.
- Minimizes to the system tray (replacing the old floating "perch" widget). Clicking the tray icon opens a small popover window; the popover can expand to the full view.
- Rooms: Home, Bedroom, Kitchen, Library, Gym, Portal, Arena, Shop. Each room owns its actions (sleep, feed, study, lift/run, expeditions, LAN battles, buying).
- Timed jobs: Study/Lift/Run 10 min, Sleep 20 min, expeditions 5–60 min by zone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pets: Dino, Lizard, Monkey, Frog, Wolf; 32px sprite sheets with 13 animation states; hats and 18 items; stats HP/Energy/Food/Mood/XP, STR/MANA/DEF/SPD.
- LAN: UDP discovery (Hello every 2 s), per-room chat, wave, gift, challenge/accept battles simulated deterministically on both sides.
- Pixel art is generated by `tools/gen_assets.py` (Pillow). All art is open to restyling: rooms, sprites' palette, icon.
- Tray on Linux runs under AppIndicator/StatusNotifier, which may not report click position or plain left-click events; the popover must have a sensible fallback position.
- Save file per OS in the user data dir; single binary per platform.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: LanPet. Pixel-art pet identity (current app icon: green dino with orange crest).

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, user counts, or metrics exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The room is the interface: where the pet is decides what you can do.
2. Glanceable first: state readable in one look, action in one click.
3. Quiet by default, lively on interaction: juice rewards attention, never demands it.
4. Coworkers' pets are the social layer; presence beats features.
